# Summary Report: "Cloud Architect"

## Opening Activity – Cloud Architect Challenge

The event started with an interactive competition called the **Cloud Architect Challenge**, which created an energetic and engaging atmosphere for all participants. A total of eight teams took part in the challenge, competing by answering questions related to cloud computing, AWS services, cloud architecture, and real-world technical scenarios.

The competition was divided into multiple rounds. After each round, the team with the lowest score was eliminated until the final winner was determined. This format encouraged teamwork, quick decision-making, and the ability to apply cloud knowledge effectively under time pressure.

## What I Learned from the Activity

Although the competition was organized as an interactive game, it provided many valuable learning experiences. Through analyzing questions, discussing possible solutions with my teammates, and listening to explanations from the organizers, I was able to:

- Review fundamental concepts of cloud computing and AWS.
- Improve my understanding of different AWS services and their practical applications.
- Enhance my critical thinking and problem-solving skills in a limited-time environment.
- Learn from the strategies and approaches used by other participating teams.

This activity also helped me recognize areas where I need to continue improving my technical knowledge and practical skills in cloud technologies.

## Personal Experience

Participating in the **Cloud Architect Challenge** was both enjoyable and meaningful. It was not simply a quiz competition but also an opportunity to practice communication, teamwork, and decision-making skills.

Working with my teammates allowed us to exchange ideas, analyze problems, and make decisions within a limited amount of time. Even when we were uncertain about some answers, the detailed explanations from the organizers helped me gain a deeper understanding of AWS concepts and their real-world applications.

The competition made the event more interactive and motivated me to continue exploring cloud technologies. It was a valuable opportunity to evaluate my current knowledge, discover new perspectives, and learn from experienced speakers as well as other participants.
