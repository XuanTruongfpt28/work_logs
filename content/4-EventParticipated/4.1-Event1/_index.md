# Summary Report: “Cloud Architect”

### Opening Activity – Cloud Architect Challenge

The event began with an interactive competition called the Cloud Architect Challenge, which created an exciting atmosphere for all participants. A total of eight teams joined the competition and competed by answering questions related to Cloud Computing, AWS services, cloud architecture, and real-world technical scenarios.

The competition consisted of multiple rounds. After each round, the team with the lowest score was eliminated until the final winner was determined. This format encouraged teamwork, quick thinking, and the practical application of cloud knowledge under time constraints.

### What I Learned from the Activity

Although the competition was designed as an engaging game, it provided valuable learning opportunities. By listening to the questions, discussing possible answers with my teammates, and reviewing the explanations provided after each round, I was able to:

Review fundamental AWS concepts and cloud computing principles.
Strengthen my understanding of various AWS services and their real-world applications.
Improve my critical thinking and problem-solving skills under time pressure.
Learn from the approaches and explanations shared by other participating teams.

The activity also helped me identify areas where I need to continue improving my technical knowledge.

### Personal Experience

Participating in the Cloud Architect Challenge was both enjoyable and educational. It was more than just a quiz competition—it required effective communication, teamwork, and quick decision-making.

Working together with my teammates allowed us to exchange ideas and make decisions within a limited amount of time. Even when our team was unsure about some questions, the explanations provided by the organizers helped me better understand the underlying concepts and practical applications of AWS services.

The competition also made the event more interactive and motivated me to continue learning cloud technologies. It was a great opportunity to evaluate my current knowledge while gaining new insights from experienced speakers and other participants.
