> Here, you can freely share your personal opinions about your experience participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program. This will help the FCAJ team improve any shortcomings based on the following aspects:

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**

Throughout my participation in the First Cloud AI Journey program, I experienced a professional yet friendly learning and working environment. Everyone was always willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties, even with issues beyond the assigned tasks. The comfortable atmosphere encouraged me to express my ideas confidently and take the initiative to learn. This is one of the aspects I appreciated the most about the program.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**

The mentors closely followed my learning progress and task completion. Whenever I faced challenges, they provided detailed guidance instead of simply giving me the answers, allowing me to understand the root of each problem and develop my own problem-solving skills. The admin team was also very responsive in providing learning materials, answering questions, and sharing important announcements, making it easy to stay on track throughout the program.

**3. Relevance to My Academic Major**

The practical assignments and projects were highly relevant to my Information Technology background and my interest in Cloud Computing. I had the opportunity to work with AWS services, GitHub, Docker, and real-world deployment workflows. These experiences allowed me to gain practical knowledge that goes beyond what I had previously learned in university courses.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**

The program provided excellent opportunities to improve both technical knowledge and soft skills. Besides learning AWS and related technologies, I also enhanced my ability to conduct self-study, manage my time effectively, collaborate with teammates, write technical documentation, and present my work professionally. These skills will be valuable for my future career.

**5. Culture and Teamwork**

One of the most impressive aspects of the program was the collaborative culture. Everyone was willing to share knowledge and support each other regardless of their role. There was no barrier between mentors, administrators, and interns, making communication open and comfortable. Workshops and knowledge-sharing sessions also gave me the opportunity to learn from experienced professionals.

**6. Policies and Support for Interns**

The program offers strong support for students through well-organized learning materials, workshops, a clear learning roadmap, and numerous hands-on projects. The flexible schedule also allowed me to balance my university studies with the internship, making the program highly suitable for students.

### Additional Feedback

The aspect I appreciated the most was being part of a practical learning environment with a clear roadmap. I not only learned theoretical knowledge but also had the opportunity to complete hands-on labs, build projects, and deploy applications on AWS. My mentors were always available to provide support while still encouraging me to research independently and strengthen my problem-solving abilities.

### Suggestions & Expectations

I would recommend organizing more networking and interaction activities between mentors and interns. These activities would provide additional opportunities to exchange knowledge, share experiences, and build stronger connections within the community.

### Additional Comments

I would like to express my sincere appreciation to the entire First Cloud AI Journey team for creating such a high-quality learning program for students. Throughout the program, I not only gained valuable AWS knowledge but also developed a more professional mindset and stronger self-learning skills. I hope the program continues to grow, expands its practical learning opportunities, and helps even more students begin their journey in Cloud Computing.
