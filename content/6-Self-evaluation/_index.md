### Needs Improvement

Although I achieved many valuable learning outcomes during the internship, I believe there are still several areas where I can continue improving:

Improve my communication skills, especially when presenting ideas and discussing technical topics with teammates.
Strengthen my problem-solving ability by approaching issues from multiple perspectives and proposing more effective solutions.
Expand my knowledge of AWS services and related cloud technologies beyond the internship requirements.
Continue improving my technical writing skills so that documentation is clearer, more organized, and easier to understand.
Develop stronger time management skills to handle multiple tasks more efficiently in future projects.

Overall, the internship at First Cloud AI Journey was a meaningful experience that helped me grow both technically and professionally. It provided me with valuable practical experience and strengthened my confidence in pursuing a career in Cloud Computing.
